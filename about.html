About FrameHouse

A studio built around your story

FrameHouse is a studio-rental destination for creators, podcasters, musicians, agencies and production teams. Book a professionally prepared room for livestreams, interviews, podcasts, music sessions and branded video. Choose the space, confirm your equipment list and add technical support to suit the way your team works.

WHO WE ARE

A home for ideas, voices and visual stories

Our studio spaces are planned around real production formats: video podcasts, interviews, livestreams, webinars, music recordings and branded content. Before your session, we help you think through the room layout, number of participants, camera positions, microphone inputs, lighting and monitoring. That preparation makes the booking easier to understand and gives hosts and crew a more comfortable place to do their best work.

OUR JOURNEY

Built one great session at a time

Our company journey is guided by one practical belief: professional production should feel accessible and straightforward to organise. We bring the room, equipment options and production support into one booking conversation. Whether you are recording your first podcast or planning a recurring broadcast, we aim to make each session easier to prepare, run and repeat.
