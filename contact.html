QUICK ANSWERS

A few things you might be wondering.

Can I visit the studio before booking?+

Absolutely. Contact us to arrange a guided tour and see which room best suits your format.

Can I bring my own equipment?+

Yes. Let us know what you plan to bring so we can confirm compatibility and setup requirements.

Can I book additional production support?+

Yes. Camera operation, technical assistance and selected post-production services can be added to your booking.

How do I manage my booking and invoices?+

Use the client portal to review demo bookings, select add-ons and view invoice examples. Final booking confirmation is handled by our team.
