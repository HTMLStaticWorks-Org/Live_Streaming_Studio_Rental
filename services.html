Our Services

Production support, made simple

Choose a production service around the content you need to make: a multi-camera livestream, a video podcast, a music or voice recording, a studio interview, a creator shoot or post-production. We can help plan camera positions, audio inputs, lighting, monitoring and the technical support needed to keep the session organised.

Podcast recording

Live streaming

Plan webinars, product launches, panel discussions, virtual events and live shows in a broadcast-ready environment. Configure camera positions, preview monitors, presenter microphones and the run-of-show before the session. Optional technical support can assist with switching, audio checks, stream preparation and live-session coordination.

STUDIO FORMATS

Video & interviews

Record interviews, product demonstrations, training videos, explainers and branded campaigns in a controlled studio environment. Flexible camera framing, practical lighting and room for hosts, guests and crew help you capture a consistent look across each shoot.

EQUIPMENT & ADD-ONS

Bring the right tools into the room

Build your equipment list around the session rather than paying for items you do not need. Options can include camera bodies and lenses, studio microphones, headphones, audio mixers, soft lights, teleprompters, confidence monitors and streaming accessories. Share your format and we’ll help identify the essentials before the booking is confirmed.

PRODUCTION CREW

A steady hand behind the scenes

Add a camera operator, audio assistant or studio technician when your team needs hands-on help. Depending on the brief, support can include equipment checks, camera framing, microphone placement, lighting adjustments, monitoring and practical assistance during the recording or broadcast.

FOR TEAMS & BRANDS

Make repeat content easier to produce
